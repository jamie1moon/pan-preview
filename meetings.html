Meetings

Two rooms every week, both on Zoom, both open to anyone who wants to work the Twelve Steps. We ask everyone to have their camera on, at least while they introduce themselves; your microphone can stay muted until you choose to speak.

Like many meetings, ours met in person until the COVID pandemic in 2020, when it moved onto Zoom. We have kept it there, because it has since become international — members now join from around the world.

We are also looking for a space to hold a face-to-face meeting, and we will let you know here as soon as we have found one.

Friday 19:00 – 20:15 London time · Zoom

The Pan meeting

The Pan meeting is open to all. Whatever the addiction or compulsion — or more than one — you are welcome here.

The fellowship meeting, running for years. It opens the same way each week — a welcome, a moment of quiet, and a short reading. Then a member shares their experience, strength and hope, focused on the solution rather than the problem. There is space to share afterwards if you want it; “I’d rather just listen” is a complete and welcome thing to say.

Ten minutes at the top of the hour — 20:00 London time — are set aside for newcomers.

How do we introduce ourselves when we share? However is true for you. If you belong to another fellowship, you might simply say you are a member of a Twelve Step fellowship — or “I am cross-addicted,” or “I am an addict,” or something similar. There is no set form of words.

Can I mention my membership of other programs? Yes. There is no restriction on talking about any addiction. If you have three or four, you can talk about each and every one of them — without fear.

The Pan Twelve Step Workshop

Unlike a regular meeting, this is a workshop. We focus on the Twelve Steps themselves, not on any single addiction or compulsion. The Steps address the underlying problem, not the addiction itself — so the workshop belongs to no one addiction, and is open to anyone, whatever brought them here.

A working session on the Steps themselves, running weekly since 2024. Where the Friday meeting carries the message, the Monday workshop takes it apart and puts it to work — a reading, a topic, and discussion. Bring a pen.

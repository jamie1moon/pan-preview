Meetings

Two rooms every week, both on Zoom, both open to anyone who wants to work the Twelve Steps. We ask everyone to have their camera on, at least while they introduce themselves; your microphone can stay muted until you choose to speak.

The Friday meeting has met every week since April 2005: in person for almost fifteen years, then, like many meetings, on Zoom from the pandemic in 2020. We’ve kept it there because it’s since become international, with members joining from around the world.

We’re also looking for a space to hold a face-to-face meeting, and we’ll let you know here as soon as we’ve found one.

Friday 19:00 – 20:15 London time · Zoom

The Pan meeting

The Pan meeting is open to all. Whatever the addiction or compulsion, or more than one, you’re welcome here.

This is the main fellowship meeting. It opens the same way each week: a welcome, a moment of quiet, and a short reading. Then a member shares their experience, strength and hope, focused on the solution rather than the problem. There’s space to share afterwards if you want it. You can simply say you’d rather listen; that’s fine.

Ten minutes at 20:00 are set aside for newcomers.

How do we introduce ourselves when we share? However is true for you. If you belong to another fellowship, you might simply say you’re a member of a Twelve Step fellowship, or “I am cross-addicted”, or “I am an addict”, or something similar. There’s no set form of words.

Can I mention my membership of other programs? Yes. There’s no restriction on talking about any addiction. If you have three or four, you can talk about every one of them, without fear.

The Pan Twelve Step Workshop

Unlike a regular meeting, this is a workshop. We focus on the Twelve Steps themselves, not on any single addiction or compulsion. The Steps address the underlying problem, not the addiction itself, so the workshop belongs to no one addiction and is open to anyone, whatever brought them here.

A working session on the Steps themselves, running weekly since 2024. The Friday meeting carries the message. On Monday we work through it: a reading, a topic, and discussion. You may wish to have a pen handy.

Who is sharing

Each Friday a different member shares at the Pan meeting. They come from many different backgrounds and many different countries, and from every kind of addiction and compulsion. Whatever brought them to the Steps, they are free to talk about all of it — any addiction, each and every one — without fear.

Past speakers

Hear a share now

You don’t have to wait until meeting night. Here you can listen to members who have shared at Pan meetings, and hear for yourself what a Pan share sounds like.

A share is only recorded with the speaker’s permission, and only the speaker’s first name and last initial are given.

The first recordings will appear here soon.

Would you like to share?

Members and visiting friends are welcome to carry the message from the Pan chair. You don’t need years behind you — you need to have worked some of it and be willing to say so honestly. Offers go to and through our Chair Finder — the member who arranges who shares each week.

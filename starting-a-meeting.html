Start a Pan meeting where you are.

Every Pan meeting began with one member who wanted a meeting and simply started one.

If you would like to start a meeting — in your town, in another country, or online — this page is what we know about how to do it. There is no permission to ask for and no office to go through. Use what is here and adapt it as you need.

Three decisions

  1. Decide what rent you can afford — always remembering that we, like all Twelve Step groups, are self-supporting through our own contributions. Many meetings begin in a church hall, a community center, or a library. Some begin on Zoom, where the rent is nothing.
  2. Choose a venue — public or private, or online.
  3. Choose a meeting format. The Pan preamble, meeting format and opening prayer are in the Store, free to download. Use them as they are, or adapt them to your meeting — chair and share, reading and share, Step study.

What people need to find you

When you tell us about your meeting, send the details that help someone get through the door:

  • The day and time — with the time zone, if the meeting is online
  • The location, or the joining link
  • The format — chair and share, reading and share, Step study
  • How to get there — nearby trains and buses, parking
  • A contact — an anonymous email address, or a phone number for recovery purposes only

Tell us, and we will list it

If you start a meeting, contact us and we will list it on this site so that people can find you. If you would like to talk it through first with someone who has done it, say so, and we will put you in touch.

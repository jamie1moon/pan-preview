Ask us to send these to you

The Pan meeting materials are free to anyone carrying the message. Tell us who to send them to, tick what you would like, and we will get them to you.

Where to send them

A first name, or first name and last initial, is fine — no more than you would give at a meeting.

Which would you like?

Tick either or both.

In keeping with our tradition of anonymity, your details are held in confidence. They are used only to send what you asked for — never published, never used to identify you as a member of any fellowship, and never added to a mailing list or passed to anyone else. Ask us and we will delete them.

This is not a membership sign-up and creates no public record — it is a private request for something to be sent, nothing more.

New, and not sure

You don’t have to write anything clever. “I’d like to come to a meeting” is enough, and somebody will answer.

Offering to share your story

Members and visiting friends are welcome to share their story at a Pan meeting. Write to our Chair Finder, the member who arranges who shares each week, and tell them a little about where you are in the Steps.

Why there is no form on this page yet. A contact form needs somewhere to send to, and a mailbox that is proven to receive. Until that address exists and has been tested end-to-end, a form would silently drop the messages of people asking for help — which is worse than no form at all. This is a deliberate hold, not an omission.

Anonymity

Pan is a Twelve Step fellowship. Who you see and what you hear stays in the meeting. Nothing you write to us is shared outside the small group of members who answer, and we never publish members’ names, phone numbers or sobriety dates.

You don’t have to do anything but arrive.

If today was hard enough that you’re reading this, you’re already in the right place. Here is exactly what happens — so nothing about a first meeting has to be a surprise.

What to expect

Your first meeting, step by step.

  1. You click a link and you’re in

    If the meeting is on Zoom, we ask that your camera is on when you arrive, so everyone in the room knows each face belongs to a real person. Once you have introduced yourself, if you have good reason to turn it off, that is fine. Your microphone can stay muted until you choose to speak. No account, no form, no fee.

  2. It opens the same way each week

    A welcome, a moment of quiet, and a short reading. Familiar and steady — you’ll know the shape of it by the second time.

  3. Someone shares their story

    A member speaks about where they were and how the Steps changed it. You just listen. Most people find something of themselves in it.

  4. You are welcome to speak — or not

    There’s space to share if you want to. “I’d rather just listen” is a complete and welcome thing to say. Nobody is put on the spot.

    At the Friday meeting, ten minutes at the top of the hour — 20:00 London time — are set aside for newcomers.

  5. You leave with a next step

    A meeting to come back to, and — if you’d like one — help finding a sponsor to walk the Steps with you.

Common worries

The things people ask before their first meeting.

“My problem isn’t drink — do I belong?”

Yes. “Pan” means all. Whatever the addiction or compulsion, if you want to work the Steps, you belong in this room.

“Do I have to talk?”

No. Your microphone can stay muted for as long as you like — listening is taking part. The one thing we ask is your camera on while you introduce yourself.

“Will it cost anything?”

No. There are no dues or fees. Pan is self-supporting through its own members’ contributions.

“What if I’ve relapsed, or it’s been a bad day?”

That’s exactly who the room is for. You don’t need to be doing well to come. Come as you are.

“Is it anonymous?”

Yes. Who you see and what you hear in a meeting stays in the meeting. Use only a first name if you prefer.

There’s a meeting this week. Come to it.

You’ve read the hard part already. The next step is small: join one meeting and see.

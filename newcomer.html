What to expect

What happens at a first meeting

  1. You click a link and you’re in

    If the meeting is on Zoom, we ask that your camera is on when you arrive, so everyone in the room knows each face belongs to a real person. Once you’ve introduced yourself, if you have good reason to turn it off, that’s fine. Your microphone can stay muted until you choose to speak. No account, no form, no fee.

  2. It opens the same way each week

    A welcome, a moment of quiet, and a short reading.

  3. Someone shares their story

    A member speaks about where they were and how the Steps changed it. You just listen. Most people hear something they recognise.

  4. You’re welcome to speak, or not, as you wish

    There’s space to share if you want to. You can simply say you’d rather listen. That’s fine. Nobody is put on the spot.

    At the Friday meeting, ten minutes at 20:00 are set aside for newcomers.

  5. Before you go

    A meeting to come back to and, if you’d like one, help finding a sponsor to walk the Steps with you.

Common worries

The things people ask before their first meeting.

“My problem isn’t alcohol. Do I belong?”

Yes. “Pan” means all. Whatever the addiction or compulsion, if you want to work the Steps, you belong in this room.

“Do I have to talk?”

No. Your microphone can stay muted for as long as you like. Just listening is taking part. The one thing we ask is your camera on while you introduce yourself.

“Will it cost anything?”

No. There are no dues or fees. Pan is self-supporting through its own members’ contributions.

“What if I’ve relapsed, or it’s been a bad day?”

Then come anyway. You don’t have to be doing well to be here.

“Is it anonymous?”

Yes. Who you see and what you hear in a meeting stays in the meeting. Use only a first name if you prefer.
